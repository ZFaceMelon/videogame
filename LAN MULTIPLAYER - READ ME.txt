PHARAOH LAN FIGHT

Both computers must be connected to the same Wi-Fi.

1. Copy the entire videogame folder to your brother's computer.
2. On the host computer, double-click "Host LAN Game.bat".
3. The black window shows the host's Wi-Fi IPv4 Address (often 192.168.x.x).
4. On the other computer, double-click "Join LAN Game.bat" and enter that address.
5. If Windows Firewall asks, allow videogame_cxx.exe on Private networks.

Controls:
  Left mouse: staff smash
  Right mouse: finger projectile
  Q: ultimate
  W/A/S/D: move
  Shift: sprint
  Space twice: fly

The host controls damage and three-second player respawns. The window title
identifies which computer is HOST and which is JOINER.

LAN port: UDP 27015
